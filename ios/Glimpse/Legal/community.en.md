Glimpse is a dating app built on respect, safety and real effort. These rules keep it that way. Breaking them can get your content hidden or your account removed, permanently.

## Be real
Only post videos of yourself, from your own life. No impersonation, no stolen content, no AI-generated or deepfake media, no misleading edits. One account per person.

## Be kind
No harassment, hate, threats, stalking or pressure. "No" means no, and so does no reply. Never pressure anyone for photos, sexual content, money or personal details.

## Respect privacy
Screen recording is blocked everywhere in Glimpse, and Vibe Checks can't be screenshotted either; an attempt during a call is shown to both people and noted for the safety team. Don't share anyone's videos, messages or personal details outside Glimpse, and never film the screen with another device: that's a serious violation.

## Show up
Matches are limited for a reason. Talk to the people you match with, and end connections you're not interested in instead of ghosting.

## Keep it appropriate
No nudity or sexual content in videos or video notes. No violence, weapons or drugs. Nothing involving minors, ever: we report it to the authorities.

## No selling, no scams
No promotion, solicitation, escort services, crypto or investment pitches, or requests for money or gifts.

## Report what feels wrong
Reports are confidential and reviewed. Reporting someone also blocks them for you, and serious reports are acted on immediately. False or retaliatory reports are a violation too.

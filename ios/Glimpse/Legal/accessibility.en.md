Glimpse wants everyone to be able to meet people, and we work to make the app accessible in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and its regulations.

## What we support
VoiceOver labels on controls and cards, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, high-contrast dark design, captions from linked posts where the platform provides them, and actions available without gestures (like, pass, next clip, report).

## What we're still improving
Captions for hosted videos, and full VoiceOver support in Vibe Check calls.

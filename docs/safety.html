Safety Tips

Most people are here for the same reason you are. These habits keep dating safe.

Before you meet

Get to know them on Glimpse first; a Vibe Check is a great way to see they're real. Keep the conversation in the app until you're comfortable: we can only help with what happens here. Never send money, gifts, codes or investment details, whatever the story.

The first date

Meet in a busy public place, and get there and back on your own. Tell a friend where you'll be and who you're meeting. Keep your drink with you. Leave whenever you want; you don't owe anyone an explanation.

If something feels wrong

Trust your instincts. Report the person in the app: they won't know, you won't see each other again, and our safety team looks at serious reports right away.

Support

Write to us about anything, including privacy, safety and reports: support@lugipod.com. We usually answer within 2 business days.

Reporting someone

Every profile, chat and Vibe Check has a report button. Reports about unsafe behaviour are handled right away. If you are in immediate danger, call the police (100 in Israel).

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile and clips are removed from the service right away; some copies are kept for a limited time as described in the Privacy Policy. Can't get into the app? Write to us with your registered phone number and, once we've verified it's you, we'll delete it for you.

Subscriptions

Glimpse+ and Glimpse Gold renew through Apple. To cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

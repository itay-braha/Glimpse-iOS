Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026 · Version 2026-10-18

This Privacy Policy explains what information Glimpse collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what your rights are. The controller of the database is Itay Braha (Israel), who operates Glimpse until its company is registered ("Glimpse", "we"). Contact for privacy matters, including our privacy officer: support@glimpse.invalid. It is written in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, its amendments and the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017. For users in Israel the Hebrew version prevails if the versions differ.

You are not legally required to give us any information. Without the information marked as needed, however, we cannot provide the Service.

What we collect

Account: your mobile number (to sign you in and keep out fake accounts), and whether it is a real mobile line (checked with our SMS provider).
Profile: name, date of birth (age), gender, the genders you're interested in, age and distance preferences, a short bio, city, and your height (or your choice not to show it; a hidden height is never shown to anyone).
Videos and photos: clips you record or upload, and links to Instagram or TikTok posts you choose to add; when each clip was filmed or posted.
Sensitive information: your gender and the genders you're interested in can reveal your sexual orientation, which the law treats as information of special sensitivity. We use it only to show you and others suitable matches and to apply safety rules, and you agree to this explicitly when you sign up.
Approximate location: rounded to about 1 km, used to show people nearby and distances and to decide when your area opens. Your exact location is never stored or shown.
Messages: text, voice and video notes you send to your matches, reactions and read receipts. If you delete a message, it disappears from the chat but a private copy is kept for 30 days (longer if the conversation is reported) for safety review.
Vibe Check safety recordings: during a 3-minute Vibe Check, after you agree in the lobby, each phone records what its own camera and microphone send and a low-quality copy of the video it receives, and uploads them to our private storage while the call runs. Attempts to screenshot or screen-record a call are logged.
Activity: likes, passes, matches, how long you watch clips, which clips you skip or finish, conversation activity (when, not what), reports you make and receive, safety decisions, and a behaviour score based on how members treat each other.
Selfie verification: a short live selfie video, reviewed by a person on our team to confirm you are the person in your profile. We do not create biometric templates.
Purchases: whether you have an active subscription and its renewal dates (from Apple). We never receive your payment card details.
Device and diagnostics: device type, app version, a device-integrity signal from Apple (DeviceCheck), push notification tokens, crash reports and basic usage events.
Consents: which version of these documents you agreed to and when.

Why we use it

To create and run your account; show you profiles and show you to others; run matching, ranking and limits; enable chat and Vibe Checks; keep members safe (verification, moderation, automatic safety measures, preventing banned users from returning, investigating reports); process subscriptions; send the notifications you allow; provide support; improve and secure the Service; comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

Automated decisions: some decisions are made automatically, including which profiles you see and in what order, behaviour scores, limits, and immediate safety measures after a report (such as hiding a member or holding their messages). You can ask for a person to review a decision that affects you by writing to support@glimpse.invalid.

Optional: research and model training

Two settings, both off unless you turn them on (Settings → Your data), and you can turn them off at any time:
Help improve matching: your activity (likes, passes, watch time, match outcomes and conversation timing, never message text, videos, your name, phone number or exact location) may be used under a random code instead of your identity to train Glimpse's own matching and safety models.
Share anonymous trends: statistics about groups of at least 20 members (for example, like rates by age range and region) may be shared with research or business partners. Nothing about you individually is shared. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Other members: your profile and videos are shown to people Glimpse shows you to and to your matches; messages only to the person you're talking to. Recordings and reports are never shown to other members.
Service providers who process information for us under contract and only on our instructions: Supabase (database and hosting), Cloudflare (video storage and call relay), Twilio (SMS codes and number checks), Apple (push notifications, purchases, device checks). Their servers may be located outside Israel, including in the European Union and the United States; transfers are made in accordance with the Protection of Privacy (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001, with providers who are bound to protect the information.
Platforms you link to: if you link an Instagram or TikTok post, it is played from that platform, which may collect information under its own policy.
Law enforcement and safety: we may disclose information when required by law or a court order, or when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to prevent harm to a person, to investigate fraud or abuse, or to protect our rights.
Business transfer: if Glimpse is transferred to a company we establish, merged or sold, information may be transferred to the new operator under this policy.

How long we keep it

Profile, videos, messages and matches: until you delete them or your account. Deleted messages: 30 days, longer while a report is open. Vibe Check recordings: 30 days, longer while a report is open. Watch-time signals: 90 days. SMS and usage logs: up to 180 days. Reports, safety decisions, device bans and consent records: as long as needed to keep the community safe, prevent abuse and defend legal claims. When you delete your account we delete your profile, videos, messages and matches, except information we must keep by law or for an open safety case.

Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls (database row-level security, private storage, short-lived links), separation of sensitive tables, logging and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and the Privacy Protection Authority of a serious security incident as the law requires.

Your rights

You can: see and correct your profile in the app; ask to review the information we hold about you (section 13 of the Protection of Privacy Law) and to correct or delete it (section 14); delete your account in Settings at any time; withdraw optional consents in Settings; turn off notifications and location in iOS Settings; and object to direct marketing (we send none without your consent). Write to support@glimpse.invalid; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Privacy Protection Authority.

Age

Glimpse is for adults 18 and over. We delete accounts we learn belong to anyone younger.

Changes

We will tell you in the app about material changes and ask for your agreement where required.
